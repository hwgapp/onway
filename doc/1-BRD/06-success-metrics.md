## Product Health

- Activation: customer first order/trip, driver activated sau xác minh, active outlets theo region.
- Retention: repeat customers, driver retention, trips/orders per driver.
- Monetization: platform fee revenue, future subscription conversion, ARPU, future ads revenue.
- Quality: match time, completion/cancellation, complaint/fraud, menu accuracy, waiting time, price mismatch.
